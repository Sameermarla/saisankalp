The Beauty of Imperfect Perfection

At Sai Sankalp, every single piece tells a unique story. From the organic fibers pressed into our handmade paper crafts to the delicate embroidery on our woolen handkerchiefs and the custom-knitted smiles of our woolen dolls, we preserve time-tested skills.

By working closely with local craftspeople, we offer eco-friendly, highly tactile items that celebrate texture, detail, and genuine warmth. No two items are identical—making your choice uniquely yours.

Request a Custom Creation

Need a personalized woolen doll, a custom journal with specific pressed flowers, or monogrammed woolen handkerchiefs? Share your ideas and we will craft it for you.

The Process

Once you submit your inquiry, our lead artisan reviews your concept. We will email you sketch concepts, wool skein selections, or flower templates for your approval before we start crafting.

Artisanal Gifting

All custom orders come in premium eco-friendly gift boxes with a handwritten card detailing the materials used and the artisan who spent their time creating it.
